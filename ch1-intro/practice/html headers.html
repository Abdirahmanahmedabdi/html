HTML Headings: <h1> to <h6>

HTML has six levels of headings. They define the structure and hierarchy of your content. The higher the number, the smaller and less important the heading.

<h1> — Main Page Title

Welcome to My Website

This is the main title of the webpage, usually used once per page.

<h1>Welcome to My Website</h1>

<h2> — Section Title

About Us

Used for major sections of content under the main heading.

<h2>About Us</h2>

<h3> — Subsection Title

Our Mission

Used for subsections under an <h2> heading.

<h3>Our Mission</h3>

<h4> — Smaller Subsection

Community Goals

Used when breaking content into smaller parts within a subsection.

<h4>Community Goals</h4>

<h5> — Minor Heading

Local Outreach

Used for fine-grained topics within a section.

<h5>Local Outreach</h5>

<h6> — Smallest Heading

Last Updated: Oct 2025

This is the least prominent heading, often used for small details or footnotes.

<h6>Last Updated: Oct 2025</h6>

Best Practices

  • Use only one <h1> per page (or per sectioning root).
  • Don’t skip heading levels (e.g., don’t go from <h2> to <h4>).
  • Headings should form a logical outline for accessibility and SEO.
